### ALGO-000180: Ultimate positivity with repeated characteristic roots
- **Subfield:** Games, automata, and verification
- **Open:** Decide whether an arbitrary linear recurrence sequence is eventually nonnegative, allowing repeated roots of its characteristic polynomial.
- **Status:** Ultimate positivity is decidable for simple recurrences of all orders. The unrestricted repeated-root case remains a different open problem.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines eventual safety and long-run sign behavior.
- **Key works:** Ouaknine and Worrell (2014), *Ultimate Positivity Is Decidable for Simple Linear Recurrence Sequences*; their positivity survey and related papers.
- **Related:** Dynamical systems—asymptotic sign behavior.
